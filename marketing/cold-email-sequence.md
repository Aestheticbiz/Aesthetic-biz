# Cold email sequence → /full-fee-patients

**Destination:** `https://www.aestheticbiz.site/full-fee-patients`
**Job of every email:** earn the click. Nothing else. The email does not sell US$10,000 — the page sells the call, the call sells the build.
**Format:** plain text. No banner, no logo, no gold bar, no two-column HTML. See "Why plain text" below.
**Signature:** always a named human — Ignatius Ackermann — never "The AestheticBiz team".

---

## Why the current phpList email will not work

| Problem | Why it costs you |
|---|---|
| Designed HTML with banner + gold bar | Reads as bulk mail. Lands in Promotions, sometimes Spam. Cold B2B outreach must look like a person typed it. |
| "Your work is exceptional. Your online profile is arguing otherwise." | On the landing page this lands, because they clicked and are already invested. From a stranger in an inbox it is unearned flattery followed by an insult. Delete. |
| Three links (button, "book a conversation", "open staraesthetic") | Split attention, and multiple links raises spam scoring. One link. |
| "The AestheticBiz team" | You are selling a founder-led US$10,000 engagement. A faceless team signature contradicts the entire offer. |
| No named offer | Nothing concrete to say yes to. |

## Subject-line rules for this audience

Doctors and established owners delete marketing language on sight. Understatement is the entire edge.

- Lowercase subjects out-perform Title Case in cold B2B. They look typed, not designed.
- No exclamation marks, no "grow", no "transform", no percentages, no emoji.
- Six words or fewer. It must be fully readable on a phone lock screen.
- Never put "AestheticBiz" or "CRM Solutions" in the subject. Brand in the subject signals bulk.

## The number that actually decides this

You want 5–10 discovery calls. Working backwards on realistic cold-outreach rates:

| Step | Realistic rate | Needed |
|---|---|---|
| Bookings wanted | — | 8 |
| Click → booking | 10–20% | ~55 clicks |
| Contact → click, across all 4 emails | 6–8% | **~750–1,000 practices** |

**If your phpList has 100 contacts, no headline on earth gets you 8 bookings.** The list size is the binding constraint, not the copy. Before sending, get the list to 750+ aesthetic and med-spa practices that match the qualifier on the page.

---

## Two deliverability warnings

1. **Do not send 1,000 cold emails from `ignatius@crmsolutions.app`.** That is your business domain and your client email. Register a separate sending domain, warm it for two to three weeks, then send. Burning your primary domain's reputation is not recoverable in the timeframe you need.
2. **Send in batches of 40–60 per day, spread over hours**, not one blast. phpList will happily fire 1,000 at once and get you blocklisted.

## Recommendation for the next iteration

Email 3 is the strongest thing in this document, and the page does not yet match it.

"Book a discovery call" asks a stranger for time. **"I will show you the three places your site leaks patients"** gives them something first, costs you twenty minutes, and every person who accepts is pre-qualified and already looking at their own problems. It is also genuinely the first phase of what you sell.

Consider making the free audit the primary CTA on the landing page and demoting the discovery call to the follow-up. I would expect it to roughly double booked calls off the same list.
