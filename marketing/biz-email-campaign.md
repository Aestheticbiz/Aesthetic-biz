# AestheticBiz — outreach sequence to /biz

**Audience:** owners of established aesthetic practices (7+ years trading, real reputation, dated web presence).
**Goal of every email:** one click to `https://www.aestheticbiz.site/biz`. Nothing else.
**Voice rule:** never tell them they are failing. Tell them something excellent is being under-served.

Send Tue–Thu, 07:00 local. Plain text, from a person, no images, no logo, no unsubscribe-heavy footer design.
Stop the sequence the moment they reply.

---

## Notes

- **Subject line discipline.** No exclamation marks, no "grow your clinic," no percentages. The audience deletes marketing language on sight; understatement is the whole edge.
- **Sequence 1 and 2 carry the emotion; 3 removes risk; 4 forces the decision.** Don't reorder — proof lands wrong before the pain is real.
- **Do not mention price in email.** The US$10,000 figure belongs on the page, after the demo invitation, where it reads as qualification rather than a pitch.
- **One link per email**, same destination every time, so click data is clean.
- Replace `{{Link}}` with a tracked `https://www.aestheticbiz.site/biz?utm_source=email&utm_campaign=owner-seq&utm_content=e1` style URL per email.
