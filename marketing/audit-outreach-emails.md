# Audit outreach emails — batch 1

Send individually from `ignatius@aestheticbiz.site`. Plain text, no HTML, no
signature image. These are one-to-one emails and should look like it.

**Replace `[LINK]`** with the subdomain URL for that clinic, e.g.
`https://audit.aestheticbiz.site/boca-med-spa/`

**Rules that hold across all of them:**

- Lowercase subject lines. Six words or fewer. No brand name in the subject.
- Lead with the single most checkable observation — something they can verify in
  under ten seconds without leaving their chair.
- One genuine positive, stated plainly, not as a compliment sandwich.
- One link. Never two.
- Sign with your name only. No title, no tagline, no logo.
- If they reply "no thanks", take them off the list the same day and say so.

## Not sent — and why

Two practices were reviewed and deliberately **not** contacted.

**Body Preserve Med Spa** (Scottsdale) — "Book Your Free Consultation" in the
hero, 4.9 stars from 84 reviews shown prominently, online booking live.

**The Beauty Spot Medspa** (North Haven, CT) — Maria Liuzzi named with
MSN · APRN · FNP-BC on the homepage, 5.0 Google rating displayed, Square
booking, and a genuinely good headline in "Still you. Just more."

Sending either of them a list of five problems would have meant inventing
them, and both owners would have known within a paragraph. The credibility
lost is worth more than the two emails gained.

This is worth tracking as a rate: **two in seven sites triaged were strong
enough to skip.** Budget for roughly 30% of any batch being unusable, and treat
that as the process working rather than failing.
