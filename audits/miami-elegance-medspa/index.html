What was looked at

Where the patients leave

Each of these is a point at which somebody who wanted to book stops. They are listed in the order they cost you most.

Leak 1 · Positioning

The headline is the name of the practice.

Where it happens: The hero reads “THIS IS MIAMI ELEGANCE”, in the largest text on the page, with the practice's logo directly above it.

How the patient is lost: Everyone reading it arrived by clicking that name, so it adds nothing. The most valuable space on the site is spent confirming something the visitor already knew, in place of what the practice does and who it is for.

Basis
Observed (text) · Interpretive (impact)
Leak 2 · Message

No treatment is named on the first screen.

Where it happens: The opening screen carries the logo, the headline, the Book Now button and the video. No treatment, category or clinical service appears anywhere on it.

How the patient is lost: A patient searching for lip filler, or laser, or skin tightening cannot tell whether this practice offers it. They have to press Book Now on faith, or leave — and most will do the second. Search engines face the same problem for the same reason.

Basis
Observed
Leak 3 · Trust

Nothing on the first screen suggests who performs the treatment.

Where it happens: No practitioner is named, no qualification, licence or supervising physician is stated, and no patient review or rating appears on the first screen.

How the patient is lost: For injectables the deciding question is who is holding the needle. An elegant room answers a different question — comfort — and leaves the clinical one open. A patient who cannot resolve it here resolves it at a practice that says so plainly.

Basis
Observed
Leak 4 · Contact

There is no phone number on the first screen.

Where it happens: No telephone number appears in the header or hero on either desktop or mobile.

How the patient is lost: Booking online suits the decided patient. The undecided one — who has a question about suitability, or price, or downtime — has no way to ask, and a proportion of those would have converted on a two-minute call.

Basis
Observed
Leak 5 · Hero video

The video plays automatically, with a pause control over it.

Where it happens: The hero is an autoplaying video with a pause button visible in the lower right of the frame on mobile.

How the patient is lost: Autoplaying video consumes mobile data before a visitor has decided the site is worth it, and on a slower connection the first thing they see may be a blank frame. The exposed pause control also signals “player” rather than “background”, which reads less polished than the brand work deserves.

Basis
Observed

Limitations & methodology

This review is based on the public homepage of miamielegance.com viewed on 13 August 2026 at 1366×768 and 390×844, page source retrieved the same day, and publicly visible business information. Treatment-page depth and technical performance were not assessed and are excluded from the score rather than estimated; the total is normalised across the six categories reviewed (90 points available).

No private analytics, Google Business Profile insights, booking data or revenue figures were accessed, and none have been estimated. Leaks labelled Observed are directly visible; leaks labelled Interpretive are professional assessment and are identified as such. No legal, HIPAA, medical board or clinical compliance judgement is offered or implied — those require professional review. Every figure in the calculator is one you entered yourself.
