The thing worth thirty seconds: a patient searching for lip filler, or laser,
or skin tightening can't tell from your first screen whether you offer it. The
headline reads "This is Miami Elegance" — your own name, which everyone
arriving already knew — and no treatment is named anywhere above the fold. They
either press Book Now on faith or leave, and most do the second.

Related: there's no phone number on that screen either. Booking online suits
the patient who's decided. The one with a question about suitability or
downtime has no way to ask.

What's genuinely good: "Book Now" is a real booking action, presented with no
competing buttons — rarer than you'd think. And your hero shows your actual
treatment room and signage rather than a stock model. Patients can tell the
difference, and it's working for you.
