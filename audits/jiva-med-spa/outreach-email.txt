Hello,

I review aesthetic practice websites, and I went through jivamedspa.com this
morning expecting to write the usual list. This is a much shorter note than I
normally send, because there isn't much wrong with it.

Your first screen names your locations and your treatments, offers a free
consultation in a proper button, and carries a 4.9 rating from hundreds of
clients alongside BBB accreditation and a HIPAA badge. That's more of the job
done properly than almost any practice I look at. I scored it 78 — the highest
in this batch by a distance.

Three refinements worth the time:

Your headline names three cities. The line beneath it names five. A patient in
Boca Raton or Wesley Chapel reads a headline that doesn't include them and may
conclude you're an Ohio business.

"Top Med Spa in Two States" sits directly above "4.9 Stars from Hundreds of
Clients". The second is specific and checkable; the first is a claim you make
about yourself. A sceptical reader who discounts one tends to discount what's
beside it.

And no clinician is named on that screen. Your institutional trust marks answer
"is this a legitimate business". A named practitioner answers "who is holding
the needle" — the question that actually decides the booking.
