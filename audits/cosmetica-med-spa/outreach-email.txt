I went through cosmeticamedspa.com this morning and wrote up what I found.

Your headline is "The best med spa in Boca Raton". The difficulty with a
superlative is that a first-time patient can't check it, every competitor
claims a version of it, and it sits in the one place on the page where proof
would work harder — a named practitioner, a credential, a rating with a count.
Right now none of those appear before a visitor decides.

Worth saying: your sticky "Call Now" bar on mobile is genuinely good. Very few
practices have one, and it means a patient who decides halfway down the page
never has to hunt for a way to act. Your consultation button is well placed
too, and having Boca Raton in the headline is right for local search.
