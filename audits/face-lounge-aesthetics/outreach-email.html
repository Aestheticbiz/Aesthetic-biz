Hi Yancey,

I review aesthetic practice websites and I went through faceloungeaz.com this morning. The notes are yours to use whether or not we ever speak.

The one worth doing today: on a phone your menu renders as a single row that runs past the right edge of the screen. Only Home, Treatments and Shop are visible — Book, Memberships, About, Blog and Contact are all outside it, and there's no menu button offered instead. Book is the one that earns, and a phone visitor currently has no visible way to reach it.

Second, your first screen doesn't say whose website it is. The hero plays a close-up of a machine's control panel — 45.0 °C, a timer, "ELAPSED", "LIVE TEMP" — with no logo, no practice name, no headline and no location. There's also a dark empty panel sitting across the middle of it that looks like something that didn't load.

What makes this worth thirty minutes rather than a rebuild: the material is already written. "Hello Gorgeous. Welcome to Face Lounge Aesthetics. We are a locally owned med spa in Scottsdale, AZ, located in the Edwards Professional Park II" — naming the actual building is exactly the kind of detail that earns trust, and no invented business ever supplies it. Your treatment menu is properly grouped the way patients search, your page description names Scottsdale, the number and the free consultation, and you've got memberships, a real shop and $25 off a first service.

Almost all of it sits below the point where most visitors decide whether to stay. Moving it up is a smaller job than it sounds.
