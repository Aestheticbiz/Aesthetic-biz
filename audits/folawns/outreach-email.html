Hello,

I review medical spa websites and spent some time on folawns.com this morning. The notes are yours to use whether or not we ever speak.

Something you're already doing well: the homepage offers two separate buttons — "Book Salon Appointment" and "Book Med Spa Appointment" — so a visitor never ends up on the wrong calendar. Combined with "A Legacy of Exceptional Care Since 1994" stated right on the page, that's a stronger opening than most two-business sites manage.

Two things are missing from that same opening screen, on both desktop and mobile: there's no phone number or address visible, and no practitioner is named anywhere in the homepage text — even though it describes the medical treatments as "backed by science." For injectables and laser work, a first-time patient usually wants to know whose hands those are before anything else.
