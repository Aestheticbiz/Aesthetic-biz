Your homepage, reviewed 13 August 2026

Hi Allison,

I looked at modernemedical.com this morning. Everything below is worth checking on your own phone, because on a desktop the site looks correct — which is almost certainly why nobody has spotted it.

On a phone, your "Schedule an Appointment Online!" button only holds the words "SCHEDULE AN". The rest — "APPOINTMENT ONLINE!" — spills out below the button onto the pink background, with APPOINTMENT split across two lines. It also overlaps your menu icon. It reads as a fault rather than a button, and it's your only call to action.

Just below it, your headline runs off the right edge. "Award Winning" loses its last letter and "in Scottsdale" is cut mid-word — so the town, the one word a local patient is looking for, is chopped in half.

None of this happens at desktop width. That's the whole reason it's still there: the version you review is the working one.

What's good is worth saying. "Schedule an Appointment Online!" is better wording than most practices manage — it names the action and tells the patient they can finish without phoning. And your hero shows a real clinician with a patient rather than a stock model.
