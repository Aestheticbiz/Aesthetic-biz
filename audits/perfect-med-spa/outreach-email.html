Hi Alessandra,

I went through perfectmedspa.com this morning and wrote up what I found. Yours to use whether or not we ever speak.

The thing that struck me: "Lift, Tighten & Restore — Without Surgery" is a genuinely good headline. Six words, names the benefit, and removes the biggest fear a first-time patient has. But it's set in light grey directly over a photograph of faces, so on a phone it's close to invisible. You're getting no return on writing that's already right.

Below it, your cookie panel takes up roughly the lower half of the opening screen. Between that, the promo bar, eight contact icons and the accessibility widget, very little of the first screen is actually yours.

Worth saying what's ahead of the field: giving NYC and Long Island each their own address, phone, email and booking control is the correct answer for a two-site practice. Most give one address and leave half their catchment guessing. And "no downtime" in the opening paragraph answers one of the two questions every patient asks before booking.
